Best Computer Science Major Professors I’ve Had So Far
I’m pretty sure there’s a website for this, but I absolutely had to give a shoutout to my top professors for the last 3 terms. They’ve been great with feedback, assistance, mentorship, and most importantly… letters of recommendations.

Prof Meredith Weidenbacher- She was my ENG Comp II Professor. She was very quick to respond to emails, and any changes I needed to make, she literally updated my grade SAME DAY. Best feeling ever is when you work hard to make changes to an assignment and your grade immediately reflects.

Prof Megan Allen Black - She was my Python Instructor (Intro to Scripting). I’m extremely grateful for her. I told her my goals and she offered to mentor me and provide me some much needed guidance. Her feedback on assignments were meaningful and not fluff, and she also responded to emails when I was absolutely lost.

Prof Robert Whale- He taught my Java class (Found In App Development). This dude absolutely rocks. Hands down one of my favorite professors and let me tell you why. He grades assignments FAST AS FUCK. I’m dead serious. I can submit my assignments on Sunday when they’re due, and literally 2 days I’ll see grades posted. I love that. Something else that I truly appreciated from him was when I was in the process of moving across country, and experiencing a death on the family, he worked with me on my late assignments.

So far I’ve taken 7 classes with SNHU, but those 3 professors are absolutely MUST HAVES if you’re just getting back in college like me. Cheers 🍻 We made it to the end of another semester and a well deserved break.

I love this post! I just graduated with a BA in Communications. I have a few favorites on my list too. So if anyone will be taking these courses and you get the same instructors, you are going to be amazed with how helpful they were and the impact they can make for you as a student.
To start, I had two outstanding professors for two general courses.
IDS-404 Popular Culture with Benjamin Olsen: He is not well rated on some websites but he is fabulous with giving feedback to make your project the best. He requires scholarly resources and mentions this in most of his announcements. I really appreciated his thoughts and suggestions on where you can take your project further. He offered ideas that were outside the box. He also graded fast as hell, so it helped with each week building onto the next. His feedback was honest, which some students found brutal. If you don't have someone holding you accountable, then you slide by. I think his dedication to using scholarly sources only builds you up for your professional career and helps you with future courses.
HIS-100 Perspectives in History with Erik Johnsen: I can't say enough great things bout this professor. He graded fairly and was quick to give your feedback to use for the next module. I learned more about research in this course, that helped me with all my courses. For anyone considering this course on Sophia, I would highly recommend taking it at SNHU because most of our assignments are writing intensive. It gives you the tools to find sources.
For my major courses, I was blessed with great professors. There was only one that I bumped heads with, over a high similarity score that was mostly my resources.
COM-311 Social Media Strategy with Ed Rideout: He is in my top 3 of professors. He made the course fun and engaging. Offered great feedback with assignments and interacted with us in the discussions. This was a course I looked forward to each week. He offered so many suggestions and you could tell he was passionate about teaching.
COM-405 Personal Brand Communications with Aaron Weese: He attended SNHU and familiar with how the courses worked. Another of my top 3 favorite professors. Right off the bat, he mentioned if you have issues with time and assignments to reach out to him and he would work with you. He offered insight with weekly announcements and would answer every question you could throw at him. I love professors that have passion to help students. I wasn't sure if this course would help me on my career path, but it did give me valuable tools. Most which I gained through the professor being willing to help.
COM-321 Global Communications with Wasif Khan: Another of my top 3 professors! I was excited for this course because it dealt with global audiences and communication strategies. Each week, Professor Khan offered outside sources to build on the topic of the modules. Another professor that has a passion to tech students and it made the course exciting. He also turned our grades in promptly. I also found his feedback to be more personal and it was never a copy and paste approach.

I also took two graduate level courses as an undergrad. Wow, those were intense but both professors were amazing. Honorable mention for Donald Olsen and Jessica Jordan. My experience in both courses has me excited to start my master degree in May.
